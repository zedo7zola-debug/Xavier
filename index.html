Career Summary

Public Health Supply Chain Leadership

  • Gallium Global: Currently serves as Global Health Supply Chain Lead, advancing pharmaceutical value chains.
  • USAID Global Health Leadership: Served at the U.S. Agency for International Development (USAID) in Washington, D.C., as Supply Chain Lead within the Office of the Assistant Administrator for Global Health and in the Office of HIV/AIDS.
  • Program Oversight: Served as Contracting Officer’s Representative (COR) for the flagship USAID Global Health Supply Chain – Procurement and Supply Management (GHSC-PSM) program.

Xavier Tomsej brings extensive experience in international public health logistics, pharmaceutical supply chain management, and health system strengthening. Through Gallium Global, his work centers on advancing resilient pharmaceutical value chains.

During his tenure at the U.S. Agency for International Development (USAID), he provided technical and programmatic leadership on national supply chain modeling, network optimization, and large-scale health commodity procurement and delivery.

He has also contributed to international policy dialogues addressing the urgent need for collaboration between medicines regulatory authorities and law enforcement agencies to combat falsified and substandard medicines, as well as the role of direct facility financing in improving commodity availability.
